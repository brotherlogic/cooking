# Crispy Ground Pork & Blistered Green Bean Stir-Fry with Mushrooms & Sweet Peppers
*Adapted from Ali Slagle & Sam Sifton (NYT Cooking)*  
*Yields approx. 5 cups (5 x 1-cup meals or 2 x 2-cup + 1 x 1-cup)*

This high-heat weeknight stir-fry pairs deeply browned, crispy ground pork with blistered green beans, meaty mushrooms, and sweet red bell peppers. Inspired by NYT Cooking's classic *Stir-Fried Green Beans With Pork*, the savory soy-hoisin-sesame glaze clings tightly to every bite and holds its texture and gloss beautifully after freezing and reheating.

**Ingredients**
- 1 lb ground pork
- 10 oz fresh green beans, trimmed and cut into 1.5-inch pieces (~2.5 cups) (vegetable 1)
- 8 oz cremini or shiitake mushrooms, stems trimmed and sliced (vegetable 2)
- 1 large red bell pepper, stemmed, seeded, and cut into ¼-inch strips (vegetable 3)
- 4 scallions, thinly sliced (whites and greens separated)
- 4 garlic cloves, finely minced
- 1 tbsp fresh ginger, finely grated or minced
- 2 tbsp neutral cooking oil (e.g., sunflower, canola, or peanut), divided
- Kosher salt and freshly ground black pepper
- Cooked noodles or jasmine rice, for serving
- *Optional Garnishes:* **Crispy Shallots**, **Toasted Nut Mix**, or **Quick-Pickled Red Onions** from `TOPPINGS.md`

**Stir-Fry Sauce**
- 3 tbsp low-sodium soy sauce
- 1.5 tbsp hoisin sauce (or oyster sauce)
- 1.5 tbsp Shaoxing rice wine (or dry sherry / low-sodium chicken broth)
- 1 tbsp unseasoned rice vinegar
- 1 tsp toasted sesame oil
- 1 tsp light brown sugar
- 1.5 tsp cornstarch
- 3 tbsp water or low-sodium chicken broth
- Pinch of red pepper flakes (optional, for gentle warmth)

**Instructions**
1. **Whisk the Sauce:** In a small bowl, whisk together the soy sauce, hoisin sauce, Shaoxing wine, rice vinegar, sesame oil, brown sugar, cornstarch, water, and red pepper flakes (if using) until smooth. Set aside.
2. **Blister the Green Beans & Mushrooms:** Heat 1 tbsp neutral oil in a large wok or 12-inch cast-iron skillet over high heat until shimmering. Add the green beans and sliced mushrooms in an even layer. Let cook undisturbed for 2 minutes so they sear and blister on the underside, then toss and stir-fry for 2–3 minutes until the mushrooms are golden and the green beans are bright green. Add the red bell pepper strips and stir-fry for 2 minutes until crisp-tender. Transfer all vegetables to a plate.
3. **Crisp the Ground Pork:** Return the pan to high heat and add the remaining 1 tbsp oil. Add the ground pork, spreading it into an even layer, and season lightly with a pinch of salt and black pepper. Let sear undisturbed for 2–3 minutes until deeply browned and crispy on the bottom, then break up into bite-sized crumbles with a wooden spoon and cook for another 2–3 minutes until cooked through.
4. **Sauté Aromatics:** Push the pork to the edges of the pan (leaving rendered fat in the center). Add the scallion whites, minced garlic, and grated ginger to the center and stir-fry for 30–45 seconds until fragrant, then toss into the pork.
5. **Combine & Glaze:** Return the blistered green beans, mushrooms, and bell peppers to the pan. Give the stir-fry sauce a quick re-whisk and pour it into the skillet. Toss vigorously over high heat for 1–2 minutes until the sauce bubbles, thickens into a glossy glaze, and evenly coats the pork and vegetables. Remove from heat and fold in the scallion greens.
6. **Portion & Freeze:** Allow the stir-fry to cool completely. Divide into freezer-safe containers (five 1-cup containers, or two 2-cup containers + one 1-cup container; yields approx. 5 cups total). Label and freeze.
7. **To Serve:** Thaw and reheat in a hot skillet or microwave. Serve over freshly cooked noodles or steamed rice, topped with **Crispy Shallots** or **Toasted Nut Mix** from `TOPPINGS.md`.
