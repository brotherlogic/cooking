# Rose's Lunches & Snacks (Nut-Free)

A modular mix-and-match system for packing healthy, nut-free lunches and snacks.

---

## 🥗 4 Lunch Elements (Mains)

### 1. Turkey & Cheese Pinwheel Wraps
* **Ingredients:** Whole wheat tortillas, sliced deli turkey, cheddar cheese slices, cream cheese or hummus spread, and thin cucumber or spinach strips.
* **Prep:** Spread cream cheese thinly across tortilla, layer turkey, cheese, and veggies. Roll tightly and slice into bite-sized pinwheels.

### 2. Pesto & Mozzarella Pasta Salad
* **Ingredients:** Rotini or bow-tie pasta, nut-free basil pesto (or sun-dried tomato pesto), mini mozzarella pearls, halved cherry tomatoes, and diced cooked chicken breast or chickpeas.
* **Prep:** Boil pasta, rinse under cold water, and toss with pesto and toppings. Pack chilled.

### 3. DIY Bento / "Lunchable" Box
* **Ingredients:** Sharp cheddar or Colby Jack cheese cubes, sliced turkey or ham (rolled up), hard-boiled egg halves, and whole grain crackers.
* **Prep:** Assemble in compartmentalized bento box sections for easy self-assembly.

### 4. Thermos Hot Mains
* **Ingredients:** Pre-heated insulated thermos with options like:
  - Chicken fried rice with peas and carrots
  - Mild coconut chicken curry over rice
  - Whole wheat pasta with marinara and mini meatballs
* **Prep:** Preheat thermos with boiling water for 5 minutes, empty, add piping hot food, and seal immediately.

---

## 🍎 4 Snack Elements (Sides & Fuel)

### 1. Fresh Veggie & Dip Cups
* **Options:** Cucumber coins, carrot sticks, sugar snap peas, or mini sweet pepper strips.
* **Dips:** Hummus, nut-free ranch dip, or tzatziki.

### 2. Protein & Dairy Boosters
* **Options:** String cheese sticks, Greek yogurt pouches/cups, cottage cheese with diced peaches, or shelled edamame (steamed with salt).

### 3. Crunchy Whole-Grain Snacks
* **Options:** Mini pretzel twists, pita chips, roasted sunflower/pumpkin seeds (pepitas), or air-popped popcorn.

### 4. Fruit & Sweet Treats (Nut-Free)
* **Options:** 
  - **No-Bake SunButter Energy Balls:** Rolled oats, sunflower seed butter, honey, flaxseed, and mini chocolate chips.
  - Fresh berry cups (strawberries, blueberries, raspberries) or apple slices (tossed in lemon water to prevent browning).
  - Whole grain mini banana-oat muffins.
